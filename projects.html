Solarshare Mobile App

SolarShare is a mobile application designed to provide affordable and clean solar energy access to residents of urban apartments through a shared rooftop solar system. The app allows users to subscribe to monthly solar energy plans, monitor their individual energy usage, view billing summaries, receive real-time notifications, and make payments conveniently. By promoting shared solar solutions, SolarShare empowers communities to reduce their carbon footprint, lower electricity costs, and contribute to sustainable urban living. In Progress
